Accounts

Accounts are grouped (Cash, Accounts, Card, Debit Card, Savings…). The group type decides how the account behaves.

Credit cards

A card account splits its balance into statements using the settlement day. Balance Payable is the last closed statement that is still unpaid. Outstanding Balance is the current open statement. Record a card payment with the Pay button in the card detail.

Debit cards

A debit card has no balance of its own. Its spending is counted in the payment account you link to it, and the card shows only this month's spending.

Changing a balance

Edit an account and type the real balance. Nexo records the difference either as income/expense or as a balance correction that does not appear in statistics.
