Frequently asked questions

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. Nexo has no internet permission. Data leaves the phone only when you export or share a file yourself.

I forgot my PIN

Use "Forgot PIN?" on the lock screen. Without the PIN, the data on the phone must be erased; you can then restore a backup file you saved earlier.

Why are transfers not in my expenses?

Moving money between your own accounts is not spending. To count money moved into savings, loans or investments as an expense, mark those accounts in Transfer-Expense setting.

How do I move to a new phone?

Save a backup with Data Backup, copy the file to the new phone, and choose "Restore from a backup file" when you set up Nexo there.
