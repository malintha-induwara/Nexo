Getting started

Nexo keeps a record of your money: what came in, what went out, and where it is now. Everything is stored on this phone only.

Add a transaction

  1. Tap + on the Trans. tab.
  2. Choose Income, Expense or Transfer.
  3. Pick an account and a category, then type the amount on the number pad.
  4. Tap Save, or Continue to enter another one.

Transfers

Use a transfer when money moves between your own accounts, for example from your bank account to savings or when you withdraw cash. Transfers are not income or expense, unless the destination is marked in Transfer-Expense setting.

Repeats and installments

Tap Rep/Inst. next to the date. A repeat adds the transaction automatically on each date. An installment splits an expense into equal monthly rows; the first row carries any rounding remainder.
