Budgets

Budgets are monthly expense limits for all spending or for one category. Set them in Settings › Configuration › Budget Setting and follow them on the Total page of the Trans. tab.

The Default Budget applies to every month. Tap a month in the budget editor to change only that month. When you change the default, the new value applies from next month.

If a main category's own budget is zero, its row shows the total of its subcategory budgets.
