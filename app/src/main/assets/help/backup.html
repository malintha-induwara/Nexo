Backup and restore

Your data lives only on this phone. If the phone is lost or the app is uninstalled, the data is lost too, so keep a backup elsewhere.

  • Data Backup saves a .nexobak file wherever you choose, such as a cloud drive app or a computer.
  • Automatic backups are taken once a day when you leave the app; the 14 newest are kept on the phone.
  • Data Recovery restores a Nexo backup, or a backup made by Money Manager on Android (.mmbak).
  • Photos are not part of backups. Copy them with Export Photo Files.

Restoring replaces all current data in Nexo.
